Privacy notice / Política de privacidad

Last updated: 2026-10-27. English first; short Spanish summary at the bottom.

Who is responsible for your data

yielddial.com is run by the YieldDial team. For any privacy or data request, write to hello@yielddial.com.

What data we collect

If you subscribe to the newsletter, we collect your email address only. The signup form sends it directly to our email provider, Buttondown (Buttondown, a US-based service), which stores it and sends the emails. Buttondown may also record technical data such as the time of signup, your IP address and whether you opened or clicked emails.

This website itself does not use advertising cookies or tracking scripts, and does not store your email address.

The site uses no analytics or tracking tools.

Why and on what legal basis

To send you the weekly YieldDial email. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), given by ticking the box on the signup form. You can withdraw it at any time without affecting what happened before.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe. After that your address is deleted or kept only as long as needed to honour your opt-out or meet legal duties.

Who receives it

Buttondown, as our processor. Because it is based in the United States, your data may be transferred outside the EU/EEA; see Buttondown's privacy policy for the safeguards it uses (buttondown.com/legal/privacy). We do not sell or share your email with anyone else.

How to unsubscribe

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you are off the list. You can also write to hello@yielddial.com.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, and object to its processing. Write to hello@yielddial.com. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the AEPD (aepd.es).
